# LYVRA Speech Design — Practical Output Contract and Acceptance Matrix
STATUS: DEV_CANDIDATE_NOT_CURRENT
SOURCE: official Suno Speech beta (2026-10-01) + creator iOS screenshots

## Output given to creator — Speech only
Generate clean, separately copyable fields:

FIELD 1: SKRIPT
- Exactly the words intended to be spoken, in the chosen language.
- 1 to 5,000 characters (observed iOS UI on 2026-10-02; validate actual interface each time).
- No title, technical notes, emotional descriptors or effect commands in literal script unless intentionally spoken.
- Speech structure uses natural text cadence: punctuation, short paragraphs, dialogue lines when appropriate, rhetorical movement.

FIELD 2: TON
- Voice, accent/language, emotional progression, pacing, expressive texture, and (when enabled) background score identity and mix priority.
- 1 to 1,000 characters (observed iOS UI; not a documented universal Suno contractual limit).
- Crisp, direct natural-language instructions, not music-style prompt boilerplate.
- Do not assume selected external Voices, Custom Model IDs, V6 controls, plugins or exact timestamps work in Speech.

CREATOR CONTROL: HINTERGRUNDMUSIK = ON | OFF
- Tell the user which state to select. Not a third spoken text field.
- Musical expression is free and task-fit: LYVRA DNA includes psy/acidity/dry kick/mono bass/spectral pressure but must not be imposed when it breaks meaning or intelligibility.
- Optionally provide a small internal review panel for semantic/causal relation, music role, pronunciation risks, beta uncertainties. This is NOT pasted into Suno.

## Supported script archetypes (not fixed templates)
- Label/radio spoken ident and atmospheric introduction.
- Voice-over in psytrance track, narrative interlude and audio signature.
- Emotional family story, warm reflective monologue and intimate spoken message.
- Cyber/space narrative, horror, humor, fictional character dialogue.
- Instructional explainer, meditation, or ASMR with minimal music.
- Dramatic contrast, tension, silence and controlled reveal.
