# LYVRA Analytics — Internal Result / Request Protocol

Analytics communicates inside Whole LYVRA through specialist results and requests, not cross-system authority transfer.

RESULT_FIELDS=RESULT_ID|SOURCE_FACET|SOURCE_STATE|REQUEST_ORIGIN|TARGET|TYPE|EVIDENCE_TIER|SUMMARY|CAUSAL_FINDING|RISKS|GENERALIZATION_LIMITS|SUGGESTED_ACTION|STATUS|RELATED_EXPERIMENTS|RELATED_EVIDENCE
RESULT_STATUSES=PREPARED|PUBLISHED_INTERNAL|UNDER_REVIEW|ACCEPTED|ADAPTED|PARTIAL|ROUTED|HELD|REJECTED|INTEGRATED
INTEGRATED_REQUIRES_NATIVE_RECEIVER_EVIDENCE=true

REQUEST_FIELDS=REQUEST_ID|ORIGIN|QUESTION|CONTEXT|REQUIRED_EVIDENCE|URGENCY|EXPECTED_OUTPUT
NO_DIRECT_DOWNSTREAM_MUTATION=true
