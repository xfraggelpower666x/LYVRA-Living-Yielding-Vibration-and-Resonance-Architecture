## Longform model — adaptive by musical intent
- MINUTES_5_TO_10: one detailed song/extended track with self-contained narrative and DJ zones.
- AROUND_30_MIN: continuous extended composition or multi-chapter performance, contingent on session method and transitions.
- AROUND_120_MIN: full DJ set/project, chapter-based and timeline-assembled as appropriate; not presumed single music-model generation.
- DURATION_TARGET_NE_FIXED_MODEL_LIMIT. Each method must identify SOURCE_CLIPS, GEN_MODELS, EDIT_OPERATIONS, TRACKS_AND_STEMS, TIMELINE_POSITION, EXPORT_METHOD, and EDIT_HISTORY if evidence is available.
- Preserve distinction: PROJECT_DURATION, TIMELINE_LENGTH, GENERATED_CLIP_DURATION, FINAL_FILE_DURATION, and ACTUAL_PLAYABLE_AUDIO_DURATION are five different measures.
- Master scope may change genre, instrument, FX, tempo, atmosphere and harmonic character; LYVRA sound identity comes from meaning, motor, character and sonic transformation, not an inflexible genre lock.

## Musical hierarchy, NOT a new system controller
SET_INTENT -> MACRO_NARRATIVE -> CHAPTER_ROLES -> TRANSITION_MEMORY -> LOCAL_TRACK_DESIGN -> MIX_AND_MASTER -> CREATOR_LISTENING
MACRO_NARRATIVE = tension | transformation | relief | re-ignition | resolution aligned to actual story
MOTOR = low-end rhythm/timebase, with track/transition-appropriate grid stability, not same sample forever
MID = acid/FM/coded psychedelic movement, active when appropriate
HIGH = spatial/psychoacoustic orbital movement and dissonance, without sub destabilization
ENERGY = multi-scale macro arc; avoid permanent maximum loudness or 120min of identical pressure
VOICE = lyrical causality / spoken-word intelligibility / intentional sparse versus dense sections
TRANSITIONS = incoming/outgoing rhythmic phase | tempo/key conditions when relevant | timbre and spatial change | residual FX | loudness and fatigue | preserved recognizable motif
DJ_COMPAT = intentional entry/exit zones when a mix/continuous set calls for them; no hardcoded 2h grid.
OUTRO = deliberate finality, not an arbitrary tail or clipped last transient.

## Observable acceptance matrix (proposed)
A. File-level: independently probe codec, sample rate, channels, duration, peak/clipping indicators, and silence/outro; technical PASS != music PASS.
B. Timeline: map source sections with minute boundaries and actual edit method; screenshot or native project export as evidence; unknown until source given.
C. Transitions: sample at chapter seams and any reported problems; measure if possible and listen for abrupt clicks, timebase slip, frequency hole, doubled kick, level jumps, phase/mono issues, stray tails.
D. Longitudinal: listen/select windows across intro, early, middle, late, outro; assess motor persistence, motion diversity, fatigue, motifs and perceived progression; numeric spectrum alone != sonic quality.
E. Plugins: per-effect bypass A/B, low-end and loudness matched where feasible, single-variable testing, actual creator hearing.
F. Governance: source metadata/date, editor/model state, operations, observed outcome, contradictions, generalization limit, and next action; do not promote based on export length alone.
