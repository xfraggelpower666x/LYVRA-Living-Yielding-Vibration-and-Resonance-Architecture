## Purpose
Formalize Studio 2 as a controlled, adaptive production-conducting workflow rather than a one-shot prompt chain. This contract adds execution grammar without replacing current Track Design, Studio Production Intelligence, Initial Take Formation, Project Chat Mix Director, Production Guide Daemon, creator FX capability cards, renderer guards or real-production learning.
