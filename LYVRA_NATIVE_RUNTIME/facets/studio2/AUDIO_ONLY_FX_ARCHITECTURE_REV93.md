## Prompt-language guard

Words that may incorrectly imply an instrument must be reviewed contextually:
INSTRUMENT | PLAYABLE | POLYPHONIC | NOTE_TRIGGER | KEYBOARD | SYNTH_VOICE

Oscillator terminology is allowed only when clearly internal to an audio processor and not a note-driven instrument.
