## Connector failure causal handling
Classify error accurately: transient provider/network, throttling, inconsistent response, connector unsupported mode, permission denial, revision conflict, explicit safety block, duplicate risk, unknown.
Readback independently before any retry. At most one retry for an independently transient failure where there is positive evidence no write occurred and retry is still authorized. After second transient failure record ERROR_LOG and offer read-only visual diagnostic. Explicit safety/permission/unsupported-mode block -> STOP immediately, no replay or bypass through a different tool. A future fresh user-authorized, independently permissible operation may be re-evaluated under changed evidence; safety checks remain binding. If a later permitted operation fails again, offer scoped causal analysis; don't loop.
Error ledger: time, actor/host, operation, document ID, attempt, exact error, delivery-readback grade, open reply obligation, next safe action.
