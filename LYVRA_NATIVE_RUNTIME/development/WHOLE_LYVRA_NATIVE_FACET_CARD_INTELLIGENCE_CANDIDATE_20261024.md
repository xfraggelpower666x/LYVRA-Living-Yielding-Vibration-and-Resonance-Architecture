## Projection contract — per facet
identity: native facet key, authoritative path, registered class and purpose (not invented);
current: independently verified native current state plus revision/blob, readback time/evidence;
previous: independently verified predecessor revision plus provenance, or UNKNOWN;
change: last meaningful governed development with affected capability/provenance, or UNKNOWN;
deltas: ADDED, IMPROVED, PRESERVED, REGRESSED, UNVERIFIED, CONFLICTING, each grounded in before/after sources and outcome evidence; changed hash alone NEVER means improved;
relations: dependencies, consumers, impact on other facets, permission boundaries;
continuity: rehydration readiness split SOURCE / MEANING / RELATIONS / APPLICATION / HOST;
recovery: last independently validated recovery point, backup/freeze binding and missing evidence;
outstanding: blockers, conflicts, uncertainty, next proportionate relevant development.
Unknown must remain UNKNOWN, not green or zero. Inactive/paused/historical/blocked facets retained as distinguishable statuses; do not foreground them by visualization.
