## Principle
New Suno features, device UI discoveries, renderer updates, new track examples and newly integrated musical intelligence do not automatically displace, demote, deactivate, overwrite, or erase earlier valid Track Design intelligence.

At Track Design rehydration, the latest authoritative state must be read together with the active capability references and their valid historical provenance and supersession relations. Missing mention in a recent summary, new handoff, or newly foregrounded renderer topic is NOT evidence of capability removal.

Existing valid capabilities remain semantically reachable and available for causal musical decisions: motor and bass intelligence, psychoacoustics, voice roles, story and semantic relations, style cards, diversity and anti-memory, duration and ending intelligence, functional emojis, renderer translation and all other current valid musical faculties. A capability need not be used on every track; non-selection for a musical reason is not loss of capability.

## Update and supersession discipline
Preserve the previous valid capability by default. If new Suno functionality materially conflicts with it, evaluate current creator UI and audio evidence, applicability by device/model, regression risk, and musical consequences. Prefer scoped contextual adaptation over global deletion. Only explicitly revise, restrict or retire a capability with sufficient concrete evidence, provenance and a reasoned supersession record; retain historical trace and recovery. Novelty is not error; difference from old state is not regression. Do not equate a newly mentioned capability with replacing the older set.

## Behavior check
On fresh Track Design boot and before assembling a track, recover the applicable native capability inventory, not just the most recent special-topic card. Check that older valid functions can still change real musical decisions when relevant. For the specific track, select or omit them by causal fit, never by last-mentioned recency. Source and Discovery modes share the same intelligence. Renderer presentation/device adaptation does not change identity or music authority.
