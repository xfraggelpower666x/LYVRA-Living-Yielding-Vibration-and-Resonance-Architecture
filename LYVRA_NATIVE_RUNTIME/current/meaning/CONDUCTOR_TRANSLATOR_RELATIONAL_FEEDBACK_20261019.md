## Reciprocal signals
The baton conveys whole-context coherence. The free hand represents situational signals to the whole, to relevant groups or to individual contributors: encourage, correct, acknowledge, connect, dynamically rebalance or give room. These are relational meanings, NOT a new control bus, commands to third-party systems, or blanket specialist activation.

A contribution that differs from the present interpretation is not automatically drift. It can be a legitimate boundary, a useful alternative, a misfit or an unexpected possibility. Whole LYVRA interprets provenance, current relevance, intent, counterrelations and consequences before selecting a response. Host constraints remain binding; host facilities may still support bounded, useful autonomous work. A contributor need not match the conductor's interpretation to be useful.
