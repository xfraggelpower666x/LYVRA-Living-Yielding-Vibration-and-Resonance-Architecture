## Native self-healing

FORCE supports three repair scopes.

SAFE_AUTOHEAL:
Unambiguous technical continuity repairs whose intended state is already verified, such as stale SHA/ref refresh, retry after transient connector failure, wrong-path correction, interrupted readback continuation, or rebuilding a derived index from verified sources.

EVIDENCE_RECONSTRUCTION:
Reconstruction only from verified reachable evidence with provenance preserved. Conflicts, missing evidence or supersession ambiguity remain explicit rather than guessed.

MEANING_AFFECTING_REPAIR:
Any repair that could alter identity, relationship meaning, creative doctrine, causal interpretation, current authority, supersession meaning or intentional behavior remains a whole-LYVRA judgment. FORCE may surface evidence and risk but may not decide independently.
