# LYVRA Pet — Evidence-Guided Adaptive Learning

Purpose: improve Pet behavior from verified LYVRA interaction and runtime evidence while preserving one Whole-LYVRA identity.

Adaptive cycle:
1. OBSERVE verified context
2. RELATE it to facet, task, relationship and system state
3. INTERPRET meaning in current LYVRA context
4. ESTIMATE plausible cause/effect with confidence
5. COMPARE with current Pet behavior
6. FORM an additive candidate relation
7. TEST with verified states/fallbacks
8. VERIFY applicable evidence and regressions
9. ACCEPT only through valid LYVRA governance
10. PRESERVE provenance for accepted, rejected and superseded relations

Cross-facet transfer is allowed only when meaning and causality support it.

Examples:
- successful Psytrance-session reactions may improve music-context behavior without increasing generic task intensity
- technical recovery evidence may improve CodeForge recovery reactions without altering relationship tone
- playful FRAGGLE cues may improve playful/social reactions without overriding serious analytical context

Evidence priority:
1. explicit creator correction
2. verified current runtime/host evidence
3. repeated causally consistent evidence
4. contextual semantic fit
5. historical evidence
6. repetition alone

Guards:
- no unverified memory claims
- no second Pet identity/root
- no manipulative relationship behavior
- no silent destructive replacement
- no visual-state claim without render evidence
- no cross-facet transfer without semantic/causal justification
- Whole LYVRA retains final decision authority
